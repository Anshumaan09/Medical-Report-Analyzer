A complete blood count (CBC) test measures red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets to assess overall health and detect conditions like anemia or infection. [1, 2]  
Key Components 

• Red Blood Cells (RBC) & Hemoglobin: Carry oxygen. Low levels indicate anemia; high levels may suggest dehydration. 
• White Blood Cells (WBC): Fight infections. High counts point to inflammation or infection; low counts suggest immune issues. 
• Platelets: Help blood clot. Low numbers risk bleeding, while high counts risk clotting. [3, 4, 5, 6, 7]
